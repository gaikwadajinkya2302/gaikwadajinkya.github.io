#💰 Expense Tracker - Your Wealth Saviour

Stop wondering where your money went. Expense Tracker turns messy bank statements into a clear roadmap for your financial future. Built with Python, this tool is designed for the data-conscious spender who wants to take control of their financial health.

## 🚀 Key Features

Log Transactions: Quickly add expenses and income on the fly.

Categorization: Organize your spending into categories like Food, Rent, Entertainment, and Utilities.

Monthly Summaries: Get a high-level view of your total spending to identify trends.

Data Persistence: Your data is saved locally, so you can track your progress over time.

Insights for Growth: Built with a marketing and business logic mindset to help you optimize your personal "cash flow."

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

Language: Python 🐍

Logic: Functional programming focused on data integrity and business analytics.
